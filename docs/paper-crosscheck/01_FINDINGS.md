## A. The push button and dose confirmation (the biggest problem)

**Decision (team, Nov 4): the button is a confirmation button in the defended build.** This matches the code on the audited branch. Every place that says "no button" or "turns off the LED and buzzer" must change to the wording below. Use the same three sentences everywhere so the paper says one thing:

> **Suggested wording.** A press of the push button while a compartment was lit confirmed every lit dose as taken: the box stopped the LED, tone and voice, played "Dose confirmed.", and saved the press with its time on the microSD card. The patient's phone collected the press at its next Bluetooth connection and recorded the dose as taken, confirmed with the box button. A press with no compartment lit recorded nothing, so a dose could not be confirmed early on the box, and holding the button for 5 seconds with nothing lit started pairing.

What the code does (evidence for the wording):
- Firmware 0.4.3, `firmware/healthsync_box/healthsync_box.ino:14-15`: "press and let go (0.3–10 s): every lit dose is taken (1.5 s tone, "Dose confirmed."). Nothing lit: nothing is taken, never early (0.8 s tone)". The same file at lines 116-119 calls `schedule_take()`.
- `firmware/healthsync_box/config.h:28` defines `TONE_TAKEN_MS`, and `config.h:105` defines `CLIP_CONFIRMED` ("Dose confirmed.").
- `config.h:77`: holding the button for 5 s with nothing lit opens pairing mode.
- `firmware/healthsync_box/BLE_PROTOCOL.md:49`: the box logs `taken` (button) and `missed` (no press within 60 min) events.
- `lib/providers/patient_provider.dart:898` `recordBoxPress()` writes the dose as taken with `confirmed_via: "button"` when the phone collects the event.
- `lib/services/box_watch_service.dart:27` (commit `36995c8`, Nov 3): while a box dose is due, the phone syncs about every minute, also in the background, in every build.
